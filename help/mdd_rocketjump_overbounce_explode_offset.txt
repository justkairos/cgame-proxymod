mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_explode_offset <usercmds>
---------------------------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
+mdd_rocketjump_overbounce: shifts which of your usercmds the blast is assumed to follow when the rocket lands beyond its first step. Default 0. Only for calibration: leave it alone unless the debug output shows the blast consistently one usercmd early or late.

See mdd_rocketjump_overbounce for the whole feature.
