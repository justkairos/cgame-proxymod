---------------------------
mdd_rocketstack_offset <ms>
---------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Shifts the rocket-stack firing moment.

Shift the +mdd_rocketstack firing moment (default 0; positive fires later).

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocketstack_offset 0
    Default: impacts at the same time.

mdd_rocketstack_offset 8
    Stacked rocket one usercmd later.
