--------------
1. Information
--------------
Upper end of the randomizer behind every automated jump and shot.

Upper end of the range every automated key hold, release stagger and jump-shot press gap is drawn from. See mdd_input_stagger_min for the full description. Default 64 (8 usercmds at 125 fps).

Default: "64"   Added: 2026-10-23

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_input_stagger_max 64
    Default.

mdd_input_stagger_max 32
    Faster key releases: at most four usercmds.
