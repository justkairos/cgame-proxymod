--------------
1. Information
--------------
Also collide the prediction with doors, plats and other brush entities.

1: also trace against the solid brush entities in your snapshot (doors, plats, trains, buttons), not just the world. Costs frame rate on entity-heavy maps; set 0 there. Moving entities are traced where they were last seen.

Default: "1"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_entities 0
    Frame rate drops on a map full of doors: world only.

mdd_player_trajectory_trace_entities 1
    Default.
