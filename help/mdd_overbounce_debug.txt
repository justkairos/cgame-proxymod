--------------
1. Information
--------------
Console output for the overbounce buttons.

Console output for +mdd_overbounce_jump: pending overbounce, overbounce speed, when the jump was pressed or why not.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_overbounce_debug 1
    Check why an overbounce jump didn't happen.
