mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_delay_max <1..12>
-------------------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Longest jump-to-shot delay tried, in pmove slots.

+mdd_rocketjump_overbounce: the longest jump-to-shot delay tried, in pmove slots (8 ms each). Default 6. Longer delays give more candidates but lower jumps.

Default: "6"   Added: 2026-11-10

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of +mdd_rocketjump_overbounce; see that entry for the whole feature.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_delay_max 6
    Default.

mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_delay_max 12
    More candidates when nothing is reachable, at the cost of height.
