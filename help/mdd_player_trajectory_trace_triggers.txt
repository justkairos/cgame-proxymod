mdd_player_trajectory_trace_triggers <0|1>

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Apply jump pads and stop at teleporters in the prediction.

1: jump pads apply their real push to the prediction, and a teleporter highlights the frame that touches it and ends the prediction there (the destination isn't known to the client).

Default: "1"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_triggers 1
    Default.

mdd_player_trajectory_trace_triggers 0
    Ignore pads and teleporters.
