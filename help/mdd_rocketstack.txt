========================
BUTTON: +mdd_rocketstack
========================

1. Information
2. Tuning
3. Notes
4. Usage examples
5. See also

---------------------------
bind <key> +mdd_rocketstack
---------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Fires a rocket at the moment it would explode together with one already in flight.

While held, with the rocket launcher out and one of your rockets still in flight: every frame the proxymod predicts where and when a rocket fired RIGHT NOW would hit, and fires at the moment that impact time equals the existing rocket's impact time, so both explode together. On a fall that moment is when you pass the rocket. If you are slower than the rocket (900 ups) the moment never comes and nothing happens.

Added: 2026-10-20

---------
2. Tuning
---------
mdd_rocketstack_offset (ms, default 0) shifts the moment; mdd_rocket_debug 1 prints the numbers.

--------
3. Notes
--------
This is a hold button like +forward or +attack: bind it to a key and it is active while the key is held. Several can share one key, e.g. bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim".

Everything the proxymod does goes through the engine's own buttons (+forward, +moveup, +attack, +left, ...), so the server sees ordinary input and your physical keys keep working. Automated jump and attack presses are held a random mdd_input_stagger_min..max ms, a jump + attack pair is never sent in the same usercmd, and no timing pattern repeats within 256 pairs.

-----------------
4. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse5 +mdd_rocketstack
    Prefire two rockets down a drop, fall past them with mouse5 held: a third is stacked onto the first one you reach.

bind shift "+mdd_rocketstack;+mdd_rocketsync"
    Stack on the way down, then jump + shoot as they land.
