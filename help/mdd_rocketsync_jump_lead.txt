--------------
1. Information
--------------
Extra lead for the rocket-sync jump only.

Extra lead for the +mdd_rocketsync JUMP only (default 24), same reasoning as mdd_grenade_jump_lead: a late jump is lost, a late shot still fires.

Default: "24"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocketsync_jump_lead 24
    Default.

mdd_rocketsync_jump_lead 40
    More margin on a laggy server.
