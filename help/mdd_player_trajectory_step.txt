----------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Draw a sample every N simulated frames.

Draw a sample every N simulated frames. Jump and teleport-enter frames are always drawn. The connecting line ignores this.

Default: "10"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_step 10
    Default.

mdd_player_trajectory_step 5
    Denser samples.
