===========================

2. Usage examples

--------------------------
mdd_grenade_jump_lead <ms>
--------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Extra lead for the grenade jump only.

Extra lead for the grenade JUMP only (default 24 ms). A usercmd that reaches the server after the explosion still fires a shot, but a jump is lost because the blast has already lifted you, so the jump is biased early while the shot stays tight. Raise it if jumps get lost on a laggy server.

Default: "24"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_grenade_jump_lead 24
    Default.

mdd_grenade_jump_lead 40
    Unstable ping: give the jump more margin.
