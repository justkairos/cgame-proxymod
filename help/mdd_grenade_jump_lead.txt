===========================
CVAR: mdd_grenade_jump_lead
===========================

1. Information

--------------------------
mdd_grenade_jump_lead <ms>
--------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Extra lead for the grenade JUMP only (default 24 ms). A usercmd that reaches the server after the explosion still fires a shot but a jump is lost because the blast already lifted you, so the jump is biased early while the shot stays tight. Raise it if jumps get lost on a laggy server.
