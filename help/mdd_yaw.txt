===============
CVAR: mdd_yaw
===============

1. Information
2. Cvars

--------------------------
mdd_yaw <angle> [angle...]
--------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Marks one or more absolute yaw angles with a vertical line on screen, the yaw counterpart of mdd_pitch. E.g. mdd_yaw 90 270. Empty = off.

--------
2. Cvars
--------
mdd_yaw_ywh Y W H        line position: y (top), width, height. Default 236 1 8.
mdd_yaw_rgba R G B A     color, default .8 .8 .8 .8
