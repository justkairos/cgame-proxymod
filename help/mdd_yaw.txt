-----------------------------
mdd_yaw <angle> [<angle> ...]
-----------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Marks yaw angles on screen with vertical lines, like mdd_pitch does for pitch.

Marks one or more absolute yaw angles with a vertical line where that heading projects on screen, the yaw counterpart of mdd_pitch. Empty = off. Position and size come from mdd_yaw_ywh, the colour from mdd_yaw_rgba.

Default: ""   Added: 2026-10-29

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_yaw 90 270
    Mark north and south.

mdd_yaw 0 90 180 270
    Mark all four axis directions, e.g. to find the quadrant for a rocket or plasma overbounce.

mdd_yaw ""
    Off.
