---------------
mdd_replay_stop
---------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Stops or disarms playback.

Stops a running (or armed) playback, releases all keys and restores your cvars. Playback also stops by itself when the replay ends.

Added: 2026-10-21

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_replay_stop
    Take control back.

bind F8 mdd_replay_stop
    Panic key.
