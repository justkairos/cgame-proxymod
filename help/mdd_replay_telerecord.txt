----------------------------
mdd_replay_telerecord <name>
----------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Records a replay starting with the first usercmd after the next teleporter.

Starts recording replay <name> with the first usercmd after you exit the NEXT teleporter. The teleporter's destination is stored in the replay so mdd_replay_telestart only triggers on that same exit. The commands that were already in flight when the teleport showed up are pulled from the client's command buffer, so nothing is lost. No cheats involved. Stop with mdd_replay_stoprecord.

Added: 2026-10-21

--------
2. Notes
--------
Replays record your usercmds (movement keys, buttons, weapon, view angles) and play them back through the engine's own buttons. Files are plain text in defrag/replays/<name>.mdd.

For exact playback: com_maxfps 125 when recording and playing, timescale 1, pmove_fixed 1 with g_synchronousClients 0 (a local server with these settings is the ideal case; g_synchronousClients 1 samples your commands at the server's pace and is NOT reproducible). Hands off mouse and keys while it plays and don't hold other +mdd_ buttons. Your cl_yawspeed / cl_pitchspeed / cl_anglespeedkey are restored when it stops.

View angles are reproduced exactly by 1 ms pulses of the keyboard-look buttons. With pmove_fixed, inputs are matched per 8 ms slot the way the server rounds them. On a remote server the first ~ping ms after a teleport are already sent before the proxymod can see the teleport, so those are your own input.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_replay_telerecord room2
    Arm it, then walk into the teleporter: recording starts on the other side.

mdd_replay_stoprecord
    Stop and save.

mdd_replay_telestart room2
    Later: arm playback and go through the same teleporter.
