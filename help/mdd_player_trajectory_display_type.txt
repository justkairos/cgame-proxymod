--------------
1. Information
--------------
How the predicted trajectory is drawn.

Bitmask.

1 full bounding boxes.

2 bottom squares only (a flat footprint).

4 jump and teleport-enter frames always as full boxes.

8 a connecting line through every simulated frame.

16 static, world-pinned samples instead of flowing ones (only while you are airborne).

Default: "0b11110"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_display_type 0b11110
    Default: footprints, jump boxes, line, static.

mdd_player_trajectory_display_type 0b01000
    Line only.

mdd_player_trajectory_display_type 0b00101
    Full boxes.
