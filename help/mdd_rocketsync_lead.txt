------------------------
mdd_rocketsync_lead <ms>
------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
How many ms before the rocket impact the sync shot is fired.

Perform the +mdd_rocketsync shot this many ms before the rocket impact (default 8). Ping is compensated separately.

Default: "8"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocketsync_lead 8
    Default.

mdd_rocketsync_lead 16
    Shot arriving late: one frame earlier.
