--------------
1. Information
--------------
Colour of the frame that touches a teleporter.

Colour and opacity of the frame that touches a teleporter, each 0..1. Default 0 .5 1 .5.

Default: "0 .5 1 .5"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_trigger 0 .5 1 .5
    Default.

mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_trigger 1 1 1 .3
    Faint white.
