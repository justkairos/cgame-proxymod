================================
CVAR / BUTTON: mdd_grenade_chase
================================

1. Information
2. Notes
3. Usage examples
4. See also

-----------------------------
mdd_grenade_chase <0|1>
bind <key> +mdd_grenade_chase
-----------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Walks you onto the spot where your grenade will explode.

When active after you fire a grenade, the proxymod walks or runs you onto the spot where the grenade will explode, using movement keys only (forward / back / moveleft / moveright, walking when close). It predicts how far you still slide after the keys are released, so it stops within about 2 units of the spot. Your view is not touched; the keys are chosen relative to wherever you are looking.

Chase stops once mdd_grenade_jump has jumped, so it doesn't fight you in the air. If the explosion point is next to a wall you may end up pushing into the wall; that is harmless.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

--------
2. Notes
--------
All grenade tools act on YOUR OWN grenades, tracked from the moment they leave the launcher. The explosion time is the launch time + 50 + 2500 ms, exactly what the server uses. The explosion point is predicted by replaying the server's missile code (bounces, the 0.65 velocity loss, the come-to-rest check) against the world.

Each feature is active while its cvar is 1 OR its +mdd_grenade_<feature> button is held. With several grenades out, mdd_grenade_last picks which one is targeted.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse4 +mdd_grenade_chase
    Hold mouse4 after firing a grenade to be walked under it.

mdd_grenade_chase 1
    Always chase every grenade you fire.

bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim;+mdd_grenade_jump;+mdd_grenade_shoot"
    One key for the whole grenade jump: walk to it, look at it, jump and fire your weapon right before it blows.
