=======================
BUTTON: +mdd_rocketsync
=======================

1. Information
2. Notes
3. Usage examples
4. See also

--------------------------
bind <key> +mdd_rocketsync
--------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Jump + shot timed to your next rocket hitting the world.

While held: times a jump + shot so the shot is the last usercmd before YOUR NEXT ROCKET hits the world (floor or wall), with the jump before it. Same timing model as the grenade tools: ping compensated, mdd_rocketsync_lead (default 8) and mdd_rocketsync_jump_lead (default 24).

Typical use: prefire rockets down a drop, land, hold this: you jump and fire right as they hit.

Added: 2026-10-20

--------
2. Notes
--------
This is a hold button like +forward or +attack: bind it to a key and it is active while the key is held. Several can share one key, e.g. bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim".

Everything the proxymod does goes through the engine's own buttons (+forward, +moveup, +attack, +left, ...), so the server sees ordinary input and your physical keys keep working. Automated jump and attack presses are held a random mdd_input_stagger_min..max ms, a jump + attack pair is never sent in the same usercmd, and no timing pattern repeats within 256 pairs.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse4 +mdd_rocketsync
    Prefire a rocket down a shaft, fall after it, hold mouse4 before landing.

bind shift "+mdd_rocketstack;+mdd_rocketsync"
    Stack a rocket on the way down and sync the jump + shot at the bottom with one key.

mdd_rocketsync_jump_lead 32
    Jump getting lost: give it more lead.
