--------------
1. Information
--------------
Console output for jumpshoot, rocketsync and rocketstack.

1: timing output for +mdd_jumpshoot, +mdd_rocketsync and +mdd_rocketstack: rocket tracking, impact times, when keys were pressed, and the peak upward speed after every jump-pad launch.

2: also dumps the jump-pad entity that was touched.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocket_debug 1
    Compare peak upward speed between mdd_jumpshoot_pad_delay values.

mdd_rocket_debug 1
    See when +mdd_rocketsync pressed jump and attack against the impact time.
