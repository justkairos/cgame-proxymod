--------------
1. Information
--------------
Prints every autojump press and its real hold.

+mdd_autojump: prints every jump press (grounded, hovering just above the ground, or predicted landing; fall speed; rolled hold) and, after the release, the usercmd times of +moveup / -moveup and the real hold length. Also prints retries and the spectator / noclip / flight hold.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-23

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_autojump_debug 1
    Check the randomization, or why a jump was retried.
