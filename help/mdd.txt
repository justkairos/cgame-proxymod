1. Information
--------------
mDd client proxymod with kairos' freestyle tools. Install: put the cgame DLL into the defrag folder and set "seta vm_cgame 0". Type "mdd_" and press tab in the console to list everything.

Help on any name below: "mdd_help <name>" or defrag's "? <name>" (both read system/help/<name>.txt; leave out the + of a button). Every help file ends with usage examples. docs/reference.html in the repository is the same content as a searchable page.

----------------
2. Grenade tools
----------------
mdd_grenade_chase                            Walks you onto the spot where your grenade will explode.
mdd_grenade_aim                              Keeps your view on the predicted explosion point of your grenade.
mdd_grenade_jump                             Jumps in the last usercmd before your grenade explodes.
mdd_grenade_shoot                            Fires your current weapon in the last usercmd before your grenade explodes.
mdd_grenade_sync                             One jump + shot timed to your grenade hitting a grenade button.
mdd_grenade_sync_offset                      How long after the first grenade touches the button the sync shot should land.
mdd_grenade_last                             With several grenades out: target the one that explodes next (0) or last (1).
mdd_grenade_lead                             How many ms before the explosion the grenade jump / shot is performed.
mdd_grenade_jump_lead                        Extra lead for the grenade jump only.
mdd_grenade_debug                            Console output for the grenade tools.

------------------------
3. Jump and rocket tools
------------------------
+mdd_jumpshoot                               Jump and fire as soon as you are on the ground with the weapon ready; fires off jump pads too.
mdd_jumpshoot_pad_delay                      Where the rocket impact should land relative to touching a jump pad.
mdd_jumpshoot_debug                          Prints the real key timing of every ground jump-shot.
+mdd_rocketsync                              Jump + shot timed to your next rocket hitting the world.
mdd_rocketsync_lead                          How many ms before the rocket impact the sync shot is fired.
mdd_rocketsync_jump_lead                     Extra lead for the rocket-sync jump only.
+mdd_rocketstack                             Fires a rocket at the moment it would explode together with one already in flight.
mdd_rocketstack_offset                       Shifts the rocket-stack firing moment.
mdd_rocket_debug                             Console output for jumpshoot, rocketsync and rocketstack.

-------------------------
4. Rocket jump overbounce
-------------------------
+mdd_rocketjump_overbounce                   One rocket jump at your current view, chosen so that you land on an overbounce.
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_nudge              Largest pitch change allowed right before the shot.
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_delay_max          Longest jump-to-shot delay tried, in pmove slots.
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_explode_offset     Calibration: shifts which usercmd the blast is assumed to follow for far-landing rockets.
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_debug              Console checks, RESULT line and rjob.log for every use.

-------------------------
5. Overbounce and jumping
-------------------------
+mdd_overbounce_jump                         Jumps on the first usercmd after an overbounce landing gave you its speed.
+mdd_overbounce_zero                         Brings your horizontal velocity to exactly 0 in the air, for a vertical overbounce.
mdd_overbounce_debug                         Console output for the overbounce buttons.
+mdd_autojump                                Presses jump every time you land, in the landing usercmd itself.
mdd_autojump_stagger_min                     Shortest hold of an autojump press.
mdd_autojump_stagger_max                     Longest hold of an autojump press.
mdd_autojump_debug                           Prints every autojump press and its real hold.

---------------
6. Input replay
---------------
mdd_replay_record                            Records your input into a replay, starting from a saved position.
mdd_replay_telerecord                        Records a replay starting with the first usercmd after the next teleporter.
mdd_replay_stoprecord                        Stops recording and writes the replay file.
mdd_replay_start                             Puts you at a replay's start position and plays its input.
mdd_replay_telestart                         Plays a replay starting when you exit the teleporter it was recorded from.
mdd_replay_stop                              Stops or disarms playback.
mdd_replay_debug                             Console output about replay sync points.

---------------
7. Input timing
---------------
mdd_input_stagger_min                        Lower end of the randomizer behind every automated jump and shot.
mdd_input_stagger_max                        Upper end of the randomizer behind every automated jump and shot.
mdd_input_debug                              Prints the real usercmd times of automated key presses.

8. HUD
------
mdd_yaw                                      Marks yaw angles on screen with vertical lines, like mdd_pitch does for pitch.
mdd_yaw_ywh                                  Position and size of the mdd_yaw lines.
mdd_yaw_rgba                                 Colour of the mdd_yaw lines.

9. Player trajectory
--------------------
mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes           Which predicted trajectories to draw (also the on/off switch).
mdd_player_trajectory_display_type           How the predicted trajectory is drawn.
mdd_player_trajectory_frames                 How many frames ahead to predict.
mdd_player_trajectory_step                   Draw a sample every N simulated frames.
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_entities         Also collide the prediction with doors, plats and other brush entities.
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_triggers         Apply jump pads and stop at teleporters in the prediction.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_passive           Colour of the passive model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_strafebot         Colour of the strafebot model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_accel             Colour of the accel model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_jump              Colour of jump frames of any model.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_trigger           Colour of the frame that touches a teleporter.
mdd_player_trajectory_shader_line            Shader used for the connecting line.
mdd_player_trajectory_shader_box             Shader used for the boxes and footprints.
mdd_player_trajectory_debug                  Developer output for the trajectory prediction.

------------------
10. Usage examples
------------------
mdd_help mdd_jumpshoot
    Help for the +mdd_jumpshoot button.

? mdd_grenade_sync
    The same through defrag's own help command.

bind mouse2 +mdd_jumpshoot
    Buttons are bound like +attack.

mdd_grenade_jump 1
    Cvars are set like any other.
