--------------
1. Information
--------------
Shortest hold of an autojump press.

+mdd_autojump: the hold time of every jump press is drawn at random from mdd_autojump_stagger_min..mdd_autojump_stagger_max ms. Defaults 8 and 64. The hold is extended if needed so the key is still down in the landing usercmd.

Default: "8"   Added: 2026-10-23

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_autojump_stagger_min 8; mdd_autojump_stagger_max 64
    Defaults: 1 to 8 usercmds.

mdd_autojump_stagger_min 24; mdd_autojump_stagger_max 24
    Always exactly three usercmds (no randomization).

mdd_autojump_debug 1
    Print the rolled and the real hold of every press.
