CVAR: mdd_grenade_lead
======================

1. Information
2. Usage examples

---------------------
mdd_grenade_lead <ms>
---------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
How many ms before the explosion the grenade jump / shot is performed.

Perform the grenade jump / shot this many ms earlier than the predicted explosion (default 8 = one server frame at sv_fps 125; negative is allowed). Your ping is already compensated separately, this is the fine adjustment.

Default: "8"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_grenade_lead 8
    Default: one server frame before the blast.

mdd_grenade_lead 16
    Shots arriving after the explosion: go one frame earlier.

mdd_grenade_debug 1
    Print the timing of every jump and shot while you tune it.
