======================
CVAR: mdd_grenade_lead
======================

1. Information

---------------------
mdd_grenade_lead <ms>
---------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Perform the grenade jump/shot this many ms earlier than the predicted explosion (default 8 = one server frame at sv_fps 125, negative is allowed). Your ping is already compensated separately, this is the fine adjustment.
