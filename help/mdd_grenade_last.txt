CVAR: mdd_grenade_last
======================

1. Information
2. Usage examples
3. See also

----------------------
mdd_grenade_last <0|1>
----------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
With several grenades out: target the one that explodes next (0) or last (1).

With several of your grenades alive: 0 (default) targets the grenade that explodes NEXT, 1 targets the one that explodes LAST. Affects chase, aim, jump and shoot.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_grenade_last 1
    Fire three grenades, act on the last one.

bind f "toggle mdd_grenade_last"
    Flip between next and last with a key.
