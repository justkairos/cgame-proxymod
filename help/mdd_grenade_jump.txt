CVAR / BUTTON: mdd_grenade_jump
===============================

1. Information
2. Timing
3. Notes

----------------------------
mdd_grenade_jump <0|1>
bind <key> +mdd_grenade_jump
----------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Jumps in the last usercmd before your grenade explodes.

When active, jumps right before your grenade explodes. With mdd_grenade_shoot also active the order is jump, shot, explosion.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

---------
2. Timing
---------
The usercmd is timed so the SERVER runs it right before the explosion: your ping is subtracted automatically, mdd_grenade_lead (default 8 = one server frame) is subtracted on top, and mdd_grenade_jump_lead (default 24) moves only the jump earlier still. A late jump is lost (the blast has already lifted you), a late shot still fires, which is why the jump gets the extra margin. You can't jump while holding your own jump key.

--------
3. Notes
--------
All grenade tools act on YOUR OWN grenades, tracked from the moment they leave the launcher. The explosion time is the launch time + 50 + 2500 ms, exactly what the server uses. The explosion point is predicted by replaying the server's missile code (bounces, the 0.65 velocity loss, the come-to-rest check) against the world.

Each feature is active while its cvar is 1 OR its +mdd_grenade_<feature> button is held. With several grenades out, mdd_grenade_last picks which one is targeted.

-----------------
4. Usage examples
-----------------
bind space +mdd_grenade_jump
    Hold space after firing a grenade at your feet: you jump just before it blows.

bind shift "+mdd_grenade_jump;+mdd_grenade_shoot"
    Grenade + rocket jump on one key.

mdd_grenade_jump_lead 32
    Jumps getting lost on a laggy server: jump a little earlier.
