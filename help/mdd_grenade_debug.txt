--------------
1. Information
--------------
Console output for the grenade tools.

1: prints when a grenade starts being tracked, which mover it will touch, and when the jump / shot is pressed, with cmd time, explosion time, ping, lead and frame step. Use it to tune mdd_grenade_lead.

2: also prints why a sync shot is being held back.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_grenade_debug 1
    Watch the timing lines while tuning the leads.

mdd_grenade_debug 2; condump grenade
    Capture everything about a sync that didn't fire.
