--------------
1. Information
--------------
Position and size of the mdd_yaw lines.

Where the mdd_yaw lines are drawn: y of the top edge, line width and line height, in the 640x480 virtual screen. Default 236 1 8 (a short tick at the crosshair).

Default: "236 1 8"   Added: 2026-10-29

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_yaw_ywh 236 1 8
    Default tick at crosshair height.

mdd_yaw_ywh 0 1 480
    Full-height lines.

mdd_yaw_ywh 200 2 80
    Thicker, taller marks.
