1. Information
--------------
Colour of the passive model's samples.

Colour and opacity of the passive model's samples, each 0..1. Default .5 .5 .5 .5.

Default: ".5 .5 .5 .5"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_passive .5 .5 .5 .5
    Default.

    Faint white.
