--------------
1. Information
--------------
Copies the console, a cvar's value or a command's output to the Windows clipboard.

clipboard alone copies everything that is in the console (the whole scrollback, not only what is on screen). clipboard <number> copies the last <number> lines; a line the console had to wrap counts as one.

clipboard <cvar> copies the value of the cvar, exactly as stored (colour codes included).

clipboard <command> runs the command and copies what it printed; everything after the first word is passed on to the command. clipboard <file>.cfg runs "exec <file>.cfg" and copies its output.

Wrapped lines are copied as whole lines, with line breaks only where the output really had them. A line confirms what was copied, with the number of lines and characters. The typed clipboard line and that confirmation are not part of the copy.

Added: 2026-11-25

--------
2. Notes
--------
The engine gives a cgame no access to the console, so the text is read from the engine's own "condump" (written to mdd_console.txt in the defrag folder and removed again). The dump is the console as stored: rows of the console's width, without colours, and with oDFe's time stamps if con_timestamp is on. The time stamps are cut off and rows that the console wrapped are put back together into whole lines.

Putting rows back together needs the console's width, which is measured with a marker line: "---capture#N-----" drawn across the whole console, with one dash left over on the next row. A command's output is what follows its marker. For the whole console the marker is only needed once (and again after a resolution or con_scale change).

One case can't be told apart afterwards: a line that ends within a word's length of the right edge, followed by a line starting with a word too long to have fit behind it, looks exactly like one wrapped line, and the two are joined. Lines well short of the edge are never touched.

The console only holds a limited scrollback: if a command prints more than that, its beginning is gone and nothing is returned.

At most about 2 million characters are copied; if the console holds more, whole lines are dropped from the top and a second line says how many. A cvar whose value is empty can't be told from a word that is no cvar, so it is run as a command and you get the engine's "name is ..." line. Windows only.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
clipboard
    The whole console, e.g. to paste a debug log somewhere.

clipboard 10
    The last 10 lines.

clipboard name
    Your player name, colour codes and all.

clipboard mapinfo
    The mapinfo report of the current map.

clipboard search mouse
    A command with arguments.

clipboard autoexec.cfg
    Whatever executing the config prints (errors, unknown commands).

bind F10 clipboard
    On a key.
