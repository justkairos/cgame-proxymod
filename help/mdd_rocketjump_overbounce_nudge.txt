1. Information
--------------
+mdd_rocketjump_overbounce: the largest pitch change it may make right before the shot to reach an overbounce; the view is turned back afterwards. Default 2. 0 = never touch the view (an overbounce is then reachable only about a third of the time). Aiming straight down needs more: 4 is a good value there.

See mdd_rocketjump_overbounce for the whole feature.
