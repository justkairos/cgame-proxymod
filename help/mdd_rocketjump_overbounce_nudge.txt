mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_nudge <degrees>
-----------------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Largest pitch change allowed right before the shot.

+mdd_rocketjump_overbounce: the largest pitch change it may make right before the shot to reach an overbounce; the view is turned back afterwards. Default 2. 0 = never touch the view (an overbounce is then reachable only about a third of the time). Aiming straight down needs more: 4 is a good value there.

Default: "2"   Added: 2026-11-10

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of +mdd_rocketjump_overbounce; see that entry for the whole feature.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_nudge 2
    Default: an overbounce is found on most presses at normal angles.

mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_nudge 4
    For straight-down rocket jumps.

mdd_rocketjump_overbounce_nudge 0
    Exact view only.
