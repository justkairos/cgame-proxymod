================================
CVAR / BUTTON: mdd_grenade_shoot
================================

1. Information
2. Timing
3. Notes
4. Usage examples
5. See also

-----------------------------
mdd_grenade_shoot <0|1>
bind <key> +mdd_grenade_shoot
-----------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Fires your current weapon in the last usercmd before your grenade explodes.

When active, fires whatever weapon you are holding right before your grenade explodes. With mdd_grenade_jump also active you jump first. Typical use: rocket + grenade jump.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

---------
2. Timing
---------
Ping is compensated automatically; mdd_grenade_lead (default 8 ms) shifts it earlier. If the weapon is still reloading at the fire time, +attack is held down until it fires (the earliest shot possible); otherwise it is a single tap.

--------
3. Notes
--------
All grenade tools act on YOUR OWN grenades, tracked from the moment they leave the launcher. The explosion time is the launch time + 50 + 2500 ms, exactly what the server uses. The explosion point is predicted by replaying the server's missile code (bounces, the 0.65 velocity loss, the come-to-rest check) against the world.

Each feature is active while its cvar is 1 OR its +mdd_grenade_<feature> button is held. With several grenades out, mdd_grenade_last picks which one is targeted.

-----------------
4. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse2 +mdd_grenade_shoot
    Fire a grenade, switch to the rocket launcher, aim at your feet and hold mouse2: the rocket goes out as the grenade explodes.

bind shift "+mdd_grenade_jump;+mdd_grenade_shoot"
    Jump and shot together on one key.

mdd_grenade_lead 16
    Shot arriving too late: fire one server frame earlier.
