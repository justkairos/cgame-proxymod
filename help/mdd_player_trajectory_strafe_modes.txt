mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes <0bXXX>
------------------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Which predicted trajectories to draw (also the on/off switch).

Bitmask of the simulated models to draw; 0 = off.

1 (0b001) passive: no input except jumps, gravity and collision only.

2 (0b010) accel: assumes you keep aiming at the same relative position inside the accel zone as now, i.e. where your jumps will land with your current input.

4 (0b100) strafebot: ideal strafing with your current keys; the theoretical best.

Default: "0b010"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0b010
    Default: your current-input prediction.

mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0b110
    Yours and the strafebot's, to compare.

mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0
    Off.
