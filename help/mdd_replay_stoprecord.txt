---------------------
mdd_replay_stoprecord
---------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Stops recording and writes the replay file.

Stops the current recording and writes defrag/replays/<name>.mdd. Recording also stops by itself after 10 minutes (buffer full).

Added: 2026-10-21

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_replay_stoprecord
    Finish the replay started with mdd_replay_record or mdd_replay_telerecord.

bind F6 mdd_replay_stoprecord
    On a key.
