======================
BUTTON: +mdd_jumpshoot
======================

1. Information
2. Notes

-------------------------
bind <key> +mdd_jumpshoot
-------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
While held: as soon as you are on the ground and your weapon is ready (weaponTime 0), jump and fire in the SAME usercmd - exactly what bind x "+moveup;+attack" does, but it waits for the ground and the reload for you. Rocket jumps, plasma hops, P-overbounces.

It re-arms every time you land again while the key stays held, so holding it chains plasma hops.

Timing: the four key events go out like a human pressing two keys - +moveup, then +attack, then -moveup, then -attack - with each gap a random mdd_jumpshoot_stagger_min..mdd_jumpshoot_stagger_max ms (default 8..64) after the previous one. The last 256 patterns (rounded to the usercmd grid, i.e. what a demo shows) are remembered and never rolled again, so no two jump-shots in a demo have identical timing. Set both to 0 for the tightest timing (consecutive usercmds). No two automated key events are ever sent in the same usercmd, so a demo never shows a +moveup;+attack-style simultaneous press or release. The jump always goes first: a shot fired before the jump explodes before a jump 10-30 ms later can register.

Jump pads: when you hit a pad that sends you up, it fires in the very next usercmd after the launch (the earliest the engine allows - the pad is applied after that command's weapon code), without a jump. Weapon must be ready. The pad replaces your velocity, so knockback only helps if the rocket explodes AFTER the launch: the proxymod predicts when you enter the pad and when a rocket fired now would hit next to it, and fires so the rocket impact lands mdd_jumpshoot_pad_delay ms relative to the touch (negative = before it). Both times are predicted from your real velocity against the real trigger brush, so the value carries over between pads and approach speeds. If no such shot is possible (not looking at the pad) it fires in the usercmd right after the launch instead. Tune it with mdd_rocket_debug 1, which prints the peak upward speed after every pad launch. If the jump doesn't take (e.g. you are holding your own jump key) it retries after 150 ms.

--------
2. Notes
--------
These +/- commands are hold buttons like +forward or +attack: bind them to a key and the feature is active while the key is held. Several can share one key, e.g.

bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim;+mdd_grenade_jump;+mdd_grenade_shoot"

Everything the proxymod does is done through the engine's own buttons (+forward, +moveup, +attack, +left, ...), so the server sees ordinary input and your physical keys keep working. Nothing touches the mouse except the aim/replay features, and those use the keyboard-look commands with cl_yawspeed/cl_pitchspeed (restored afterwards).

All automated jump/attack presses are held for mdd_input_time ms (default 32) plus/minus mdd_input_time_randomize ms (default 12) so they show on input huds in demos.
