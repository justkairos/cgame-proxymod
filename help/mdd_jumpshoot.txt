BUTTON: +mdd_jumpshoot
======================

-------------------------
bind <key> +mdd_jumpshoot
-------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Jump and fire as soon as you are on the ground with the weapon ready; fires off jump pads too.

While held: as soon as you are on the ground and your weapon is ready, it jumps and fires - what bind x "+moveup;+attack" does, but it waits for the ground and the reload for you and spreads the key events like a human. Rocket jumps, plasma hops, P-overbounces. It re-arms every time you land again while the key stays held, so holding it chains plasma hops. If the jump doesn't take (e.g. you are holding your own jump key) it retries after 150 ms.

Added: 2026-10-20

---------
2. Timing
---------
The four key events go out as +moveup, then +attack a random mdd_input_stagger_min..max ms later, then -moveup, then -attack a random stagger after it. The holds are random too and no pattern repeats within the last 256 jump-shots. No two automated key events are ever in the same usercmd. mdd_jumpshoot_debug 1 prints the rolled gaps and the real usercmd times of all four events.

------------
3. Jump pads
------------
When you are about to hit a pad that sends you up, it fires without a jump. It predicts when you enter the pad and when a rocket fired now would hit next to it, and fires so the rocket impact lands mdd_jumpshoot_pad_delay ms relative to the touch (negative = before it). Both times come from your real velocity against the real trigger brush, so the value carries over between pads and approach speeds. If no such shot is possible (not looking at the pad) it fires in the usercmd right after the launch. mdd_rocket_debug 1 prints the peak upward speed after every pad launch.

--------
4. Notes
--------
This is a hold button like +forward or +attack: bind it to a key and it is active while the key is held. Several can share one key, e.g. bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim".

Everything the proxymod does goes through the engine's own buttons (+forward, +moveup, +attack, +left, ...), so the server sees ordinary input and your physical keys keep working. Automated jump and attack presses are held a random mdd_input_stagger_min..max ms, a jump + attack pair is never sent in the same usercmd, and no timing pattern repeats within 256 pairs.

-----------------
5. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse2 +mdd_jumpshoot
    Hold mouse2 with the rocket launcher, looking down: a rocket jump the moment you can.

bind mouse2 +mdd_jumpshoot
    Hold it with the plasma gun against a wall to chain plasma hops.

mdd_jumpshoot_pad_delay -8
    Falling onto a jump pad: have the rocket land one usercmd before you touch it.

mdd_jumpshoot_debug 1
    Check the randomized key timing of every jump-shot.
