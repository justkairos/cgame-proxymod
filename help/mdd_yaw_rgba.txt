--------------
1. Information
--------------
Colour of the mdd_yaw lines.

Colour and opacity of the mdd_yaw lines, each 0..1. Default .8 .8 .8 .8.

Default: ".8 .8 .8 .8"   Added: 2026-10-29

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_yaw_rgba 1 0 0 1
    Solid red.

mdd_yaw_rgba .8 .8 .8 .4
    Faint grey.
