--------------
1. Information
--------------
Prints the real usercmd times of automated key presses.

1: after every automated jump + attack pair has been released, prints the usercmd times of the four events, how long each key was held, the press gap and the release gap (and the gap that was rolled), whatever feature sent them.

2: also prints every single automated tap (pad shots, autojump, lone jumps) with its press and release times.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-23

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_input_debug 1
    Verify the stagger of jump + shot pairs before recording a demo.

mdd_input_debug 2
    Include single taps.
