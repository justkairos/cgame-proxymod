--------------
1. Information
--------------
How many frames ahead to predict.

Number of simulated frames (8 ms each). Default 270, capped at 2000. Cost grows with it.

Default: "270"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_frames 270
    Default, a little over two seconds.

mdd_player_trajectory_frames 125
    One second: cheaper.
