--------------
1. Information
--------------
Prints the real key timing of every ground jump-shot.

+mdd_jumpshoot ground jump-shots: prints the gap that was rolled and, once both keys are back up, what was actually sent: the usercmd times of +moveup, +attack, -moveup, -attack, how long each key was held, the gap between the two presses and the gap between the two releases. Use it to confirm the stagger and randomization for a demo. Pad shots are reported by mdd_rocket_debug.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-23

-----------------
2. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_jumpshoot_debug 1
    Hold +mdd_jumpshoot for a while and read the [input] lines.

mdd_input_stagger_min 8; mdd_input_stagger_max 16; mdd_jumpshoot_debug 1
    Narrow the stagger and confirm the holds really stay within one or two usercmds.
