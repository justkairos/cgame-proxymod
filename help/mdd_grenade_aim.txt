---------------------------
mdd_grenade_aim <0|1>
bind <key> +mdd_grenade_aim
---------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Keeps your view on the predicted explosion point of your grenade.

When active after you fire a grenade, the proxymod keeps your view aimed at the predicted explosion point (within about 0.25 degrees). It uses +left / +right / +lookup / +lookdown with cl_yawspeed and cl_pitchspeed set per frame; your original values are restored when it stops. Mouse input still adds on top and is corrected the next frame.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-20

--------
2. Notes
--------
All grenade tools act on YOUR OWN grenades, tracked from the moment they leave the launcher. The explosion time is the launch time + 50 + 2500 ms, exactly what the server uses. The explosion point is predicted by replaying the server's missile code (bounces, the 0.65 velocity loss, the come-to-rest check) against the world.

Each feature is active while its cvar is 1 OR its +mdd_grenade_<feature> button is held. With several grenades out, mdd_grenade_last picks which one is targeted.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
bind mouse5 +mdd_grenade_aim
    Hold mouse5 to look at where your grenade is going to explode.

bind shift "+mdd_grenade_aim;+mdd_grenade_shoot"
    Look at the explosion point and fire a rocket into it at the last moment.
