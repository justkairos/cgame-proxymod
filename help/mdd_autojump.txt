=====================
BUTTON: +mdd_autojump
=====================

1. Information
2. Timing
3. Notes
4. Usage examples
5. See also

------------------------
bind <key> +mdd_autojump
------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Presses jump every time you land, in the landing usercmd itself.

While held, presses jump every time you are on the ground or about to land, so the jump goes out in the landing usercmd itself (what a frame-perfect manual jump does). Works in VQ3, where holding your own jump key does nothing, and in CPM.

Added: 2026-10-23

---------
2. Timing
---------
The jump key goes down a little before the landing (at least two usercmds, or half the rolled hold) and stays down until the engine has actually jumped; it does not have to hit one exact usercmd. That is what makes it reliable next to ledges and walls, where the real move slides or steps and lands a usercmd earlier or later than a straight line predicts, and with pmove_fixed, where one usercmd in three runs no pmove at all and a key that is only down for that one is never seen. Each press is down for a random mdd_autojump_stagger_min..mdd_autojump_stagger_max ms in total (default 8..64), longer if the jump takes longer to come. If no jump comes within 300 ms (right after spawning the engine refuses jumps until attack is released) the key is let go and pressed again.

If you are hovering a fraction above walkable ground without being grounded (sticky overbounce) the key is held until one lands. With the Flight powerup, as a free spectator or in noclip it simply holds jump down for as long as the key and that state last.

Together with +mdd_overbounce_jump on the same key, nothing is pressed ahead of a landing (a jump key that is down when an overbounce lands cancels it): normal landings are then jumped one usercmd after touching down, overbounce landings by +mdd_overbounce_jump.

--------
3. Notes
--------
mdd_autojump_debug 1 prints every press and, once released, the exact usercmd times. Movers (func_plat etc.) are not part of the landing prediction: on those the jump goes out one usercmd after landing.

This is a hold button like +forward or +attack: bind it to a key and it is active while the key is held. Several can share one key, e.g. bind shift "+mdd_grenade_chase;+mdd_grenade_aim".

Everything the proxymod does goes through the engine's own buttons (+forward, +moveup, +attack, +left, ...), so the server sees ordinary input and your physical keys keep working. Automated jump and attack presses are held a random mdd_input_stagger_min..max ms, a jump + attack pair is never sent in the same usercmd, and no timing pattern repeats within 256 pairs.

-----------------
4. Usage examples
-----------------
bind space +mdd_autojump
    Hold space to bunny-hop: every landing is jumped on its own usercmd.

bind space "+mdd_overbounce_jump;+mdd_autojump"
    Also catch overbounce landings.

mdd_autojump_stagger_min 16; mdd_autojump_stagger_max 48
    Keep the jump key down 2-6 usercmds per press.

bind space +mdd_autojump
    In spectator or noclip the same key climbs.
