=============================
COMMAND: mdd_replay_telestart
=============================

1. Information
2. Chaining
3. Notes

----------------------------------------
mdd_replay_telestart <name> [<name> ...]
----------------------------------------

--------------
1. Information
--------------
Plays a replay starting when you exit the teleporter it was recorded from.

Loads replay <name> and plays it back starting with the first usercmd after you exit the SAME teleporter it was recorded from. Teleports landing elsewhere (other teleporters, respawns) are ignored and it stays armed. No cheats needed. Meant for replays made with mdd_replay_telerecord; a replay made with mdd_replay_record has no exit position stored and triggers on the next teleport of any kind. Stop with mdd_replay_stop.

Added: 2026-10-21

-----------
2. Chaining
-----------
Several names can be given (up to 16): they play back to back; only the first waits for the teleporter.

--------
3. Notes
--------
Replays record your usercmds (movement keys, buttons, weapon, view angles) and play them back through the engine's own buttons. Files are plain text in defrag/replays/<name>.mdd.

For exact playback: com_maxfps 125 when recording and playing, timescale 1, pmove_fixed 1 with g_synchronousClients 0 (a local server with these settings is the ideal case; g_synchronousClients 1 samples your commands at the server's pace and is NOT reproducible). Hands off mouse and keys while it plays and don't hold other +mdd_ buttons. Your cl_yawspeed / cl_pitchspeed / cl_anglespeedkey are restored when it stops.

View angles are reproduced exactly by 1 ms pulses of the keyboard-look buttons. With pmove_fixed, inputs are matched per 8 ms slot the way the server rounds them. On a remote server the first ~ping ms after a teleport are already sent before the proxymod can see the teleport, so those are your own input.

-----------------
4. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_replay_telestart room2
    Arm it and walk into the teleporter; the recorded input takes over on the other side.

mdd_replay_telestart room2 room3
    Chain a second replay straight after the first.

mdd_replay_stop
    Disarm it again.
