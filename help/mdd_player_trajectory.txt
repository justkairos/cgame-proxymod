--------------
1. Information
--------------
Draws your predicted path as a row of hitboxes: where you will be over the next frames with your current input, including jumps, jump pads and collisions. By akuzmichov (written 2026-08-28, merged into this fork 2026-10-27).

mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes is the on/off switch (0 = off). Each cvar below has its own help file; the full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

--------
2. Cvars
--------
mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes           "0b010"        Which predicted trajectories to draw (also the on/off switch).
mdd_player_trajectory_display_type           "0b11110"      How the predicted trajectory is drawn.
mdd_player_trajectory_frames                 "270"          How many frames ahead to predict.
mdd_player_trajectory_step                   "10"           Draw a sample every N simulated frames.
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_entities         "1"            Also collide the prediction with doors, plats and other brush entities.
mdd_player_trajectory_trace_triggers         "1"            Apply jump pads and stop at teleporters in the prediction.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_passive           ".5 .5 .5 .5"  Colour of the passive model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_strafebot         "0 .5 0 .5"    Colour of the strafebot model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_accel             ".4 .4 0 .4"   Colour of the accel model's samples.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_jump              ".8 0 0 .8"    Colour of jump frames of any model.
mdd_player_trajectory_rgba_trigger           "0 .5 1 .5"    Colour of the frame that touches a teleporter.
mdd_player_trajectory_shader_line            "railCore"     Shader used for the connecting line.
mdd_player_trajectory_shader_box             "bbox_nocull"  Shader used for the boxes and footprints.
mdd_player_trajectory_debug                  "0"            Developer output for the trajectory prediction.

-----------------
3. Usage examples
-----------------
mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0b010
    On, showing where your current input takes you (default).

mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0b110
    Add the strafebot's ideal path to compare against.

mdd_player_trajectory_frames 125; mdd_player_trajectory_trace_entities 0
    Cheaper: one second ahead, world collision only.

mdd_player_trajectory_strafe_modes 0
    Off.
