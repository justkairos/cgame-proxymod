--------------
1. Information
--------------
Developer output for the trajectory prediction.

Verbosity level, not needed for normal use. 1 every simulated frame, 2 the next predicted jump frame, 3 the strafebot steering chain once per jump, 4 the first 10 frames every render frame, 5 the gathered entities and triggers. Any nonzero level also prints a timing line. Capture with \logfile 2.

Default: "0"   Added: 2026-10-27 (written 2026-08-28 by akuzmichov)

--------
2. Notes
--------
Part of the predicted player trajectory by akuzmichov (merged from github.com/akuzmichov/cgame-proxymod, branch player_trajectory). The full reference is docs/mdd_player_trajectory.md in the repository.

3. Usage examples

    Off.
